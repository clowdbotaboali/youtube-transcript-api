# Transcripta GTM Playbook (14 Days)

## 1) Primary Goal
- Close first 5 paying customers.
- Time window: 14 days.
- Focus on one channel only: direct outreach.

## 2) Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
- YouTube agencies that manage long-form channels.
- Course creators with 30+ minute educational videos.
- Podcast teams repurposing episodes into written content.

## 3) Offer Positioning
- Core promise: "Turn long YouTube videos into searchable text + execution-ready summaries in minutes."
- Risk reversal: free pilot on one video.
- Paid conversion path: current standard plan on the website.

## 4) Daily Activity Targets
- 30 new outbound messages per day.
- 10 personalized follow-ups per day.
- 1 to 2 short demos per day.
- End-of-day KPI update in tracking sheet.

## 5) Outreach Templates

### First Message (DM/Email)
Subject: Quick idea for your long YouTube videos

Hi {{FirstName}},

I watched your content and noticed you publish long-form videos.
I run a tool that converts YouTube videos into transcript text + structured summaries in minutes.

If useful, I can process one of your videos for free and send the output today.

Open to test it?

### Follow-up 1 (24h)
Hi {{FirstName}},

Just checking if you want me to run a free sample on one of your recent videos.
No setup needed from your side, only a URL.

### Follow-up 2 (72h)
Last ping, {{FirstName}}:
If transcript + summary automation is not a priority now, no problem.
If it is, send one link and I will return a free sample.

## 6) Demo Flow (10 Minutes)
- Minute 1: confirm current workflow and pain.
- Minute 2-5: run one live URL.
- Minute 6-8: show transcript + summary + practical use case.
- Minute 9-10: close with clear next step (trial to paid).

## 7) Objection Handling
- "We already use YouTube captions."
  - Reply: Captions are not workflow-ready. We provide reusable text and structured output immediately.
- "No budget now."
  - Reply: Start with one free sample and compare time saved before deciding.
- "Not urgent."
  - Reply: Understood. I will share one sample so you can evaluate when needed.

## 8) Tracking Sheet Columns
- Date
- Lead Name
- Company/Channel
- Segment (Agency/Course/Podcast)
- Contact Method (DM/Email)
- Status (Sent/Reply/Demo/Paid/Lost)
- Last Action Date
- Next Follow-up Date
- Pain Point
- Offer Sent (Y/N)
- Outcome

## 9) Weekly KPI Targets
- 200 to 250 first messages sent.
- 30 to 40 positive replies.
- 8 to 12 demos.
- 3 to 5 paid customers.

## 10) Non-Negotiables
- Do not start paid ads before first 5 paying users.
- Do not change pricing every day.
- Do not target multiple personas at once in the same 14-day sprint.
